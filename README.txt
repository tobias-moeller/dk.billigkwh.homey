Add danish energy prices from Strømligning to homey, make flows and automate your smart devices acording to el prices.
You can change net company and el company in advanced settings under device. 
Turn on price forecasts in the device settings to get estimated prices up to 7 days ahead.
Set the resolution to quarter in the device settings to also get prices per 15 minutes.
Turn on the sell price in the device settings to see what solar power sells for. It is the spot price excl. VAT minus the production tariffs. Your electricity supplier's buy-back terms are not included.
The app was called BilligKWH until version 2.6.0.
Data provided by Strømligning. https://stromligning.dk
